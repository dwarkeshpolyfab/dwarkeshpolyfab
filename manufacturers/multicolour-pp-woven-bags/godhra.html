❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Godhra Supply)

Can Dwarkesh Polyfab deliver Multicolour PP Bags directly to our facility in Godhra?
Yes. We arrange direct factory dispatch from our Morbi plant to Godhra using dedicated Full Truckloads (FTL) for large consignments or scheduled Part Truckload (PTL) carriers with direct door delivery to your warehouse.
How is road transport freight calculated for Godhra?
Road freight from Morbi to Godhra is billed on actual weight at approx. ₹2.10 per KG via major transport carriers. On a standard order of 25,000 bags, freight adds approximately ₹0.19 per bag.
What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
Our standard MOQ for Godhra dispatch is 10,000 Bags. We also provide sample swatches and trial test bags prior to bulk production.
Can we print our brand logo and custom graphics?
Yes. We offer high-definition flexographic and rotogravure printing up to 6 to 8 colors with custom cylinder engravings matching your brand guidelines.
