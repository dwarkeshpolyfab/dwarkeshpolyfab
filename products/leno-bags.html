Features of PP Leno Bags

  • Open Net Vegetable Ventilation: 360-degree continuous air circulation dispels field heat and moisture, preventing crop rot and sweating.
  • High Tensile Knotted Columns: Figure-eight twisted warp columns lock straight horizontal weft rows to eliminate mesh slippage.
  • Reinforced Side Border Strips: Colored selvage vertical side borders (blue, purple, red) absorb lifting tension and prevent seam tearing.
  • Folded Top Mouth Hemming: Double-stitched top hem designed for automatic sewing machines or hand sewing in packhouses.
  • High Capacity Export Rating: Special double mesh category engineered for 70 kg to 80 kg loading of cashews, potatoes, and onions.
  • High-Definition Flexo Printing: Print brand logo, net weight, crop grade, and barcodes in up to 6 colors on a center laminated band.
  • Advanced UV Weatherometer Protection: Fortified with 1.0%–2.0% HALS UV stabilizers for open port storage and solar exposure.
  • 100% Food Grade & Recyclable: Made from non-toxic virgin Polypropylene (PP) safe for fresh produce contact.
