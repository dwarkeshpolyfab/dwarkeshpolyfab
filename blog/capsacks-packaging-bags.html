Capsacks (often searched as cap sacks or jumbo master woven sacks) are extra-large polypropylene woven bags built specifically for bulky, low-density products where volume matters more than raw weight. Instead of expensive corrugated boxes that eat up transport space and cost ₹80–₹150 per carton, manufacturers pack plastic wares, bottle caps, jugs, toys, and foam items directly into lightweight 40–80 GSM PP capsacks.

Common Uses of Cap Sacks Across Industries

  • Plastic Bottle & Cap Moulders: Blow-moulded PET bottles, HDPE gallon cans, screw caps, and closures.
  • Plastic Household & Toys: Buckets, mugs, toys, kitchen containers, and lightweight consumer goods.
  • Textile, Hosiery & Garment Bundles: Master outer sacks protecting packed cartons during pan-India road transport.
  • Bric-a-Brac & Export Cargo: High-volume dust protection for mixed export items and light freight.
