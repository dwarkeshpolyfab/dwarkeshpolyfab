1. What is an Open Net Leno Bag & Why Does Produce Need It?

A Leno Bag is a breathable woven mesh container made from high-tenacity virgin Polypropylene (PP) or High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE). Unlike solid PP woven sacks or plastic bags, Leno bags feature an open-mesh grid structure that permits 360-degree continuous air ventilation.

💡 The Physics of Zero Crop Rot in Fresh Produce

Freshly harvested produce such as onions, potatoes, garlic, and citrus release moisture through natural respiration. When packed in solid bags, trapped heat and condensation trigger fungal rot, sprouting, and weight loss. Open Net Leno Bags dispel field heat instantly, extending shelf life by up to 45 days in mandi transport and cold storage.

2. Weaving Mechanics: Straight Weft Rows vs. Knotted Warp Columns

Not all leno bags are created equal. Low-cost market bags often suffer from "mesh gaping," where horizontal and vertical threads slide apart when loaded with 50 kg of heavy potatoes. At Dwarkesh Polyfab, we engineer our sacks using Dense Interlock Weaving Mechanics:

  • ➖ Straight Horizontal Rows (Weft Tapes): Flat polymer ribbons run completely straight horizontally, spreading lateral crop weight across the entire bag width.
  • 🪢 Knotted Vertical Columns (Warp Pairs): Dual vertical warp threads twist in a figure-eight interlock knot around every horizontal row, locking each grid intersection permanently.
  • 🎨 Reinforced Selvage Side Border Strips: High-density colored side borders (Blue, Purple, Red) absorb vertical lifting tension, shielding side seams from tearing under heavy crane and container loading.
