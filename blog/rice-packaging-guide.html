Why Rice Packaging Bag Choice Matters

The packaging bag is the first thing a rice buyer sees. In wholesale markets, a plain strong PP bag does the job. But in retail stores, supermarkets and export markets — the bag is your brand. A poor quality or dull-looking rice bag can push buyers to competitors even if your rice quality is better.

Choosing the right bag also protects your rice from moisture, pests and breakage during transport and long-distance shipping — directly affecting the quality your customer receives and your reputation as a supplier. For export via Mundra Port or Kandla Port, the bag must handle sea-freight humidity and rough container handling.

Should You Add an Inner PE Liner?

An inner LDPE (polyethylene) liner is a thin plastic sleeve placed inside the woven bag before filling. It creates a fully sealed moisture barrier from all sides. For rice being exported via Mundra Port or stored in Gandhidham warehouses during the humid season — inner liner is strongly recommended. It adds a small cost per bag but significantly reduces spoilage, moisture damage claims and customs rejection risk at the port of destination.

Rice Export Bags: What Importers Demand

If you are exporting rice to UAE, Bangladesh, Africa, Sri Lanka or East Asia, your packaging must meet specific requirements that international buyers check before accepting a shipment:

  • Food-grade certification: Virgin PP only — no recycled content
  • Moisture barrier: BOPP lamination or inner LDPE liner for sea freight
  • Weight accuracy marking: Net weight printed clearly — 25kg / 50kg
  • Country of origin: "Made in India" printed per importing country requirements
  • FSSAI number: Mandatory for rice exported from India
  • Bag strength: Drop test compliant — no breakage during port handling
  • Stitch quality: Double-stitch bottom seam — prevents burst on stacking
Mundra Port Note: Mundra handles over 60% of India's basmati rice export volume. If your rice is loading from Mundra, your packaging bags must be at Gandhidham warehouse before the loading date. With Morbi-based supply, you can get bags to Gandhidham within 1 day — giving you maximum flexibility on export scheduling.

Printing Tips for Export & Retail Rice Bags

  • Print brand name in large bold font — visible from 3 metres in a warehouse or store
  • Mention rice variety clearly — Basmati, Sona Masoori, PR-14, IR-64, Ponni etc.
  • Print net weight and FSSAI license number (mandatory in India)
  • For export: add country of origin and importer's name/address
  • Include WhatsApp number for repeat buyer contact — works very well for wholesale bags
  • Use appetizing grain photography on BOPP laminated bags — increases retail shelf pickup
  • QR code linking to your brand page — growing trend in premium rice packaging

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best bag for 25 kg rice?

For 25kg rice packaging, an 85–90 GSM food-grade PP woven bag with BOPP lamination is the best choice. It gives strength for heavy handling, moisture resistance for storage and professional brand printing for retail and export.

Can plain PP bags be used for rice export?

Plain PP bags can be used for bulk export of parboiled or non-basmati rice where cost is the priority. However, for basmati rice and premium varieties, BOPP laminated bags with full print are strongly preferred by international importers — especially in UAE and Gulf markets.
