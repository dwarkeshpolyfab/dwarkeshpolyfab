How to Calculate Exact Landed Price Per Bag

Understanding landed cost ensures transparent procurement without hidden distributor markups. The mathematical formula for landed cost is:

Landed Cost / Bag = [Factory Unit Price] + [Bag Weight (in KG) × Freight Rate (per KG)] + GST

Frequently Asked Questions on Pan-India Factory Dispatch

1. Why is buying PP woven bags directly from a Morbi, Gujarat factory cheaper than local dealers?
Gujarat is the primary manufacturing hub for polymer processing in India with direct access to petrochemical refineries (Reliance Jamnagar, IOCL, ONGC Petro additions). Buying direct from our Morbi factory eliminates 12% to 18% intermediary trader markups, while bulk road logistics costs only ₹1 to ₹4.50 per kg across major Indian industrial corridors.
2. How is the landed cost of a PP woven bag calculated?
Landed cost per bag = Direct Factory Unit Price + (Bag Weight in kg × Road Freight Rate per kg). For example, a 100g 50kg bag costing ₹12.00 at factory with ₹3.00/kg road freight to Mumbai adds just ₹0.30 per bag in freight, resulting in a ₹12.30 landed cost.
3. What is the transit time for Full Truckload (FTL) dispatches across India?
